Biography

In my own words

I started in networks. My first jobs were building and running enterprise network infrastructure for a systems integrator and then in the IP core of Wateen Telecom, one of Pakistan’s first fibre operators. That is where I learned that the systems people rely on are only as good as the discipline behind them.

From 2010 I moved into public service, leading IT for the Lahore Transport Company and then the Lahore Parking Company, where I automated parking with number-plate recognition and built a geofenced subsidy system for public transport. In 2015 I became Director IT at Pakistan Railways, and later returned for a second term to lead its SAP S/4HANA transformation and e-ticketing.

In between, I went to France. I completed a PhD in Computer Science at Université Paris Cité on scaling blockchain systems, and worked as a security engineer for Alfabet-group and Zyneas Technology, running vulnerability management and building CVE tooling for commercial security operations. I remain an associate researcher at the university’s LIPADE laboratory, and I review for international journals.

Back in Pakistan I led IT for Education Services Limited, The City School Network, where we launched the organisation’s first online bookstore and a customer-care platform, and taught digital forensics at Lahore Garrison University. Since August 2026 I am General Manager IT at the Punjab Agriculture, Food and Drug Authority.

Alongside all of it I consult, I write Python, and I build things: a staff portal, a continuous security audit, a live CRM for a solar company, research simulators, and retrieval-augmented AI assistants. I also supervise student teams building their first RAG applications. I work in English and Urdu, and get by in French.

Dr Fahad Rahman is an independent IT and cybersecurity consultant, Python developer and blockchain researcher based in Lahore, Pakistan, with ties to Paris, France. Technology strategy and digital transformation, cybersecurity assessment and governance, ERP and enterprise systems (SAP S/4HANA, Oracle EBS, Microsoft Dynamics 365), infrastructure and network design, Python development and automation, AI and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, blockchain advisory, and training workshops.

Government and public-sector regulators, national railways, public transport and parking authorities, education, telecom and multinational organisations, as well as private companies such as a solar energy installer.

Yes. He co-authored a 2024 book chapter on the fundamentals of blockchain and smart contracts.

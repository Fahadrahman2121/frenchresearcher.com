Blockchain

Blockchain research and projects

Making blockchain systems scale without giving up trust: sharding, cross-chain control, oracle trust and, most recently, quantum threats to the mempool. Every project has a peer-reviewed paper behind it.

Accepted 2026 · Springer

Blockchain-enabled 5G vehicular networks

Federated trust-aware sharding and cross-shard authentication, so vehicles are authenticated across shards without a central authority.

Cluster Computing (Springer), 2026 · DOI · preprint
Accepted 2026 · IEEE BCCA

ASTRA: trustless cross-chain control

A controller that keeps cross-chain batching stable when relayers drop messages or go quiet, without trusting any single party.

IEEE BCCA 2026, with L. Lahlou, F. Naït-Abdesselam and N. Kara · journal version in preparation
2026 · IEEE ICACT

Q-MEMShield: quantum-resilient mempool

Protects a transaction during the seconds it waits to be mined, when a quantum attacker could derive its key. Follow-on work bounds the exposure.

IEEE ICACT 2026, with co-authors at STC Saudi Arabia · journal extension in preparation
2023–2024 · Oracles

Trust in decentralised blockchain oracles

Asymmetric Byzantine quorums let an oracle network tolerate untrusted data sources without a single trusted aggregator.

IEEE SoftCOM 2023 and journal extension · arXiv 2401.00175
2023–2025 · Scalability

Sharded and layered blockchain architectures

Heuristic sharding for DNS load balancing, layered designs that separate consensus from execution, and optimised load balancing among shards.

IEEE BCCA 2023 · IEEE CommNet 2023 · Cluster Computing 2025
2020–2023 · Doctorate

PhD in Computer Science (Blockchain)
